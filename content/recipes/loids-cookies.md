# Loid's cookies

Spitzbuben recipe translated into English and adapted from [Swissmilk](https://www.swissmilk.ch/de/rezepte-kochideen/rezepte/RB_KAF2002_27/spitzbuben/).

Vanillekipferl recipe adapted from [The Daring Gourmet](https://www.daringgourmet.com/vanillekipferl-austrian-vanilla-crescent-cookies/).

Spritzgebäck recipe adapted from [Recipes from Europe](https://www.recipesfromeurope.com/german-spritz-cookies/).

## Linzer/Spitzbuben

### Ingredients

- 250g softened butter
- 125g powdered sugar
- 2 packets vanilla sugar OR 10g powdered sugar and 10 mL vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- 1 white from a large egg
- 350g all-purpose flour
- 200g of your favourite filling (I used a combination of raspberry jam and Nutella)

### Steps

1. In a medium mixing bowl and using a mixer, cream the butter until you can pull up the mixer. Add the powdered sugar and vanilla, and combine until the butter looks bright. Add in the flour and combine to create a dough.
2. Wrap the dough in some plastic wrap and chill for 48 hours. This concentrates the sugar.
3. Divide the dough into two equal portions. In between two lightly floured parchment papers, roll out each dough until 3mm thick (about the same thickness of two credit cards).
4. Cut out your cookies. If you don’t have a Linzer-specific cookie cutter, then use a paring knife or a toothpick to cut out designs for the “tops”.
5. Tray the cookies on parchment paper lined pans and chill for 15 minutes. This prevents the dough from spreading.
6. Bake for 6-8 minutes at 350F.
7. For each cookie, spread 2.5mL of your favourite filling on a “bottom” (bottom side up) and sandwich with a “top”. I prefer to make my cookies to order.

## Vanillekipferl

### Ingredients

Cookies:

- 250g all-purpose flour
- 227g softened unsalted butter
- 113g almond flour
- 1 package vanilla sugar OR 5 mL vanilla extract and 5 mL powdered sugar
- 90g powdered sugar
- 1.5g salt

### Steps

1. In a medium mixing bowl, add powdered sugar, vanilla, salt, and flours. Add in the softened butter and combined until you have a dough
2. Shape the dough into a log and chill for 48 hours.
3. Portion to 17-18 g pieces and shape to crescents. You can let the dough soften in your hands before shaping them into crescents. If so, chill the cookies for 15 minutes so they firm up
4. Bake at 350F for 12-15 minutes. The edges should be slightly brown. 
5. If using vanilla sugar, combine the sugar and the powder together.
6. Sift half of the powdered sugar (or powdered sugar mixture) on the freshly baked cookies. Let cool to room temperature and sift the other half of the powdered sugar on the cookies.

## Spritzgebäck

### Ingredients

- 170g softened unsalted butter
- 100g granulated sugar
- 5 mL vanilla extract OR 1 package of vanilla sugar
- 1 room temperature large egg
- 1 room temperature egg yolk
- pinch of salt
- 250g all-purpose flour
- 2g baking powder
- 100g chocolate, preferably a pre-tempered one (I like Callebaut) but you can use chocolate chips

### Steps

1. Combine the butter and the sugar in a medium mixing bowl a until creamy. (I used a hand mixer, but you could use a spoon)
2. Add the egg and egg yolk and keep beating until combined. 
3. Combine flour, baking powder, and salt, before gradually adding this flour mixture into the wet ingredients using a spoon. Keep mixing until the flour is fully mixed in. 
4. Cover with a plastic wrap and let stand for 20 minutes but no longer than two hours.
5. If you have a piping bag, fill it with your cookie dough, before slowly piping out straight lines and swirl shapes on a parchment-lined sheet pan. If you don’t have a piping bag, create patties out of the dough and draw the ridges using a toothpick. 
6. Chill the cookies for 15 minutes so the dough can firm up.
7. Bake at 350F for 12-15 minutes and let cool on a sheet pan or on a cooling rack.
8. Melt your chocolate in the microwave in 30-second intervals.
9. Carefully dip parts of the line cookies into the melted chocolate. Place on a parchment-paper lined cookie sheet, before chilling for 15 minutes so the chocolate can set. 

## Notes

### In general:
- I made my own powdered sugar using [Simply Recipe's recipe](https://www.simplyrecipes.com/how-to-make-powdered-sugar-5197799).
- I used the Costco vanilla extract.

### Spritzgebäck

- I recommend using a reusable canvas piping bag. You can get that from Michael's or Bulk Barn.
- If you don’t have a piping bag, you can shape cookie patties and draw ridges with a toothpick.
